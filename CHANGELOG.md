# Changelog

Pocket Arcade follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): the minor
version goes up for new games and features, the patch version for fixes.
The version shows at the bottom of the home screen. Entries describe what
players can see: new games and features, changes to how things play, and
anything taken away (not internal fixes or renames along the way).

## 1.89.0
- Benji's Town: a treat on the way -- tap the shop's apple or the café's
  ice cream cone, and the helper you're driving (or watching) goes in for
  one: out of the cab, into the shop, and out with an apple ("A delicious
  apple!") or an ice cream ("Yum! Ice cream!"), then on with the trip.

## 1.88.0
- Benji's Town: jobs in any order -- blue recycling bins as well as
  trash, picked up whichever way round, and the dump whenever. Tap a job
  in the tray to do it next: the camera shows the helper who'll do it,
  and its button bounces. Every step is a tap (the dog plays in the park
  till you send them home), and every helper's button stays in view.
- Benji's Town: yellow dots on the road show the way a helper's going,
  from above as from the driver's seat.
- Benji's Town: driving yourself is a switch (the car button, top right),
  kept -- not by day.
- Benji's Town: building, a step at a time ("Get some concrete for the
  foundation", "Now build the walls!"), and it says what you did: "You
  built a house! Great job!". It talks one line at a time, never over
  itself. Picking up recycling sounds like cans and paper.

## 1.87.0
- Benji's Town: it says what to do next -- "The garbage needs to be
  picked up!", "Time to deliver the mail!", "Time to take the dog for a
  walk!" -- and that helper's button bounces with a glowing ring till
  it's tapped. A moment after each part of the day begins, then now and
  then while nobody's busy.

## 1.86.0
- Benji's Town: no more levels -- one town that goes on from day to day
  (and is kept). Jobs in the morning, at midday and in the evening; then
  night falls, with the moon and stars, lights in the windows,
  streetlamps and headlights, everyone goes home, and it's the next
  morning. The sky changes with the time of day, the clock waits for the
  jobs (nobody's ever late), and the town grows as the days go by.

## 1.85.0
- Benji's Town: a big round button over each helper with a job to do,
  showing a picture of its next job -- tap it to start. The arrow to where
  it's going (and the letters and parcels the houses are waiting for)
  shows only once it's on its way.
- Benji's Town: a bigger town -- the streets three tiles apart, with room
  to queue between the corners, and a turning circle at the end of every
  street, round an island with a tree, instead of U-turns.
- Benji's Town: cars and trucks never drive into each other: they queue
  bumper to bumper, don't go into an intersection without room to get
  out, never turn across each other at the lights, and go round the block
  rather than turning round in the street. A helper with nothing to do
  that's in the way drives off home.

## 1.84.2

## 1.84.1
- Water Lab: the Map button's icon is a little patch of the lattice -- a

## 1.84.0
- Water Lab: a **Map** button (beside Hint, and on the end-of-level card)
  shows the whole solution space -- every state the water can be in, every

  shortest way on, every state cross-connected by every pour. With three
  beakers it's a hexagonal lattice: each pour is a straight line along one
  of six directions, as long as the amount poured. With four, a 3D
  lattice you turn round with your finger. Pinch to zoom, tap a state.

## 1.83.2

  13°/s. With assist off, only a slow spin (under 2°/s) ebbs away when you
  let go; anything faster keeps spinning until you counter it.

## 1.72.3
- Lunar Lander: simply gentler side thrusters, the same for you and the
  autopilot (no more progressive buttons): a quick tap is a touch of about
  0.5°/s, and big turns take a little longer. Each mission's fuel is
  worked out again to match, and the autopilot's routes fly more gently.
  Zigzag tunnels keep a steady slope, getting longer each time round
  instead of steeper.

## 1.72.2
- Lunar Lander: finer turn buttons. A quick tap now gives just a touch of
  spin (about 0.5°/s), and holding builds to full strength for big turns.
  With assist off, any spin ebbs gently away (0.5°/s every second), so a
  tap nudges you over and coasts to a stop -- no more chasing drift.

## 1.72.1

## 1.67.2

## 1.67.1

## 1.55.0

  Scrabble tiles, face down, each sounding its code (600 Hz, keyed at 20

  board with the letter you heard to clear it and the falling one; the
  tiles above drop down. Wrong letter? A copy of the right one drops in.
  Lessons bring in the short letters first, then longer ones, numbers and
  punctuation, and then speed up to 20 words a minute.
